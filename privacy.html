Privacy

Glitch Lore Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Glitch Lore YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

It uses Google's YouTube API with upload permission for the owner's own channel. It collects no data about anyone else, stores nothing except the owner's own sign-in token on the owner's computer, and shares nothing with third parties.

This stats page shows public YouTube numbers only and uses no cookies or tracking.
